
# ECOMMERCE STANDARD

## OBJECTIVE

Build trustworthy and functional shopping experiences.

## DISCOVERY

Understand:
- Product catalog.
- Customer segments.
- Markets.
- Currency.
- Tax requirements.
- Shipping.
- Returns.
- Payment providers.
- Inventory requirements.

## PRODUCT DISCOVERY

Implement where required:
- Categories.
- Search.
- Filters.
- Sorting.
- Product variants.
- Product recommendations.
- Product galleries.

## PRODUCT DETAIL

Include:
- Accurate descriptions.
- Product photography.
- Pricing.
- Availability.
- Variants.
- Specifications.
- Shipping information.
- Returns information.

## SHOPPING EXPERIENCE

Implement:
- Cart.
- Checkout.
- Customer accounts.
- Order confirmation.
- Order history when required.

## TRANSACTIONS

- Calculate prices server-side.
- Verify payment provider responses.
- Validate stock.
- Protect order operations.
- Handle pending payments.
- Handle failed payments.
- Prevent duplicate processing.

Never store raw card details.

Use established payment providers.

Use sandbox environments for testing.

Never claim that checkout works
without verifying the integration.

## TRUST

Never fabricate:
- Reviews.
- Ratings.
- Sales figures.
- Customer testimonials.
- Certifications.

## ACCESSIBILITY

Ensure product controls, forms,
filters and checkout are accessible.
