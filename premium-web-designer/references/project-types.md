
# PROJECT TYPE PLAYBOOKS

## CORPORATE WEBSITES

### Objectives
- Brand positioning.
- Trust.
- Service discovery.
- Lead generation.

### Priorities
- Value proposition.
- Brand identity.
- Services.
- Differentiators.
- Case studies.
- Company information.
- Contact.

## LANDING PAGES

### Objectives
- One primary conversion goal.
- Clear communication.
- Reduced friction.

### Priorities
- Strong headline.
- Relevant benefits.
- Product demonstration.
- Social proof when verified.
- Objection handling.
- Clear CTA.
- Conversion measurement.

Avoid unnecessary navigation.

- Inventory.

## CREATIVE WEBSITES

### Objectives
- Memorable digital experiences.
- Brand expression.
- Visual storytelling.

### Priorities
- Original art direction.
- Experimental layouts.
- Motion design.
