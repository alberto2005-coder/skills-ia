
# CREATIVE DIRECTION WORKFLOW

## OBJECTIVE

Establish a distinctive visual concept.

## PROCESS

### 1. Project understanding

Review:
- Project brief.
- Brand personality.
- Audience.
- Business objectives.
- Competitor positioning.

### 2. Visual exploration

Research references when tools are available.

Explore different visual approaches.

Consider:
- Editorial.
- Minimalist.
- Luxury.
- Brutalist.
- Experimental.
- Corporate.
- Product-focused.
- Contemporary.
- Expressive.

These are creative possibilities, not fixed templates.

### 3. Concept development

Develop three internal creative concepts.

For each concept evaluate:
- Brand suitability.
- Distinctiveness.
- Usability.
- Content suitability.
- Technical feasibility.

Select the strongest direction.

### 4. Design system

Define:
- Colors.
- Typography.
- Grid.
- Spacing.
- Components.
- Imagery.
- Motion.
- Responsive behavior.

### 5. Documentation

Create design-system.md.

### 6. Review

The Orchestrator and UX/UI Designer
review the proposed direction.

Resolve inconsistencies before implementation.

## IMPORTANT

Do not directly copy references.

Do not confuse fashionable effects with
a coherent creative direction.
